## Operator diagnostic interpretation (human-readable, privacy-safe)

- Read `classification` to identify the failure kind and `phase` to locate the current operation. Read `lastConfirmedBoundary` as the last observed action; `firstUnconfirmedBoundary` identifies where proof stopped. `effect: unknown` means **do not assume nothing happened and do not blindly retry**.
- `relay-outbox-write-failed`: command preparation succeeded, but local queue-file creation was not confirmed. Inspect local relay file state; do not assume it was published.
- `device-result-unavailable`: the controller queued a request but cannot prove the device received or executed it. Inspect pending command/receipt and reconcile before retrying.
- `result-malformed` / `result-correlation-mismatch`: an inbox entry was seen, but its contents or identifiers failed validation. Retain and inspect the suspicious record; do not treat it as success.
- `command-handler-failed` / `sequence-state-finalization-failed` / `prior-outcome-uncertain`: the agent has or may have claimed the command; action effects remain uncertain. Reconcile durable claim/outcome before any further execution.
- `mailbox-drive-unauthorized`, `mailbox-drive-forbidden`, `mailbox-drive-rate-limited`, `mailbox-drive-provider-unavailable`, `mailbox-drive-transport-unavailable`: classify an authorization, throttle, provider or network failure without exposing the request/body/token. Check credentials or connectivity from an appropriately protected environment.
- `result-persistence-failed`: primary result output is not confirmed; the secondary structured terminal warning is not proof that original evidence was saved. Check output directory, permissions, and storage.
- `invalid-scenario`: preflight rejected the scenario before any dispatch; review its schema and required fields. `bridge-unexpected-internal-error`: sanitized fallback cannot establish a specific cause; inspect protected original evidence, not raw public logs.
- Do not report device publication, reception, execution, or completion as confirmed unless the relevant authenticated record exists. The separately planned PowerShell interface consumes the existing typed diagnostic fields; it must not generate independent conclusions.
