# DEC-339 — Minimal Production Inventory Integration

**Date:** 2026-11-17; **alignment correction:** 2026-11-17  
**Status:** Owner-directed **planning only**. Dormant inventory software is on `master`; the shipping runtime still uses the legacy assembler and IndexedDB v1 state adapter. **No** implementation dispatch, live pairing/database migration, Drive/vault activity, physical testing, installation, activation or release is authorized here.

## Goal and existing components

Use the existing **device-local metadata inventory for every in-scope LOCAL file/folder and its known managed REMOTE counterpart**. Verified remote metadata may feed the current snapshot assembler; persisted local metadata is **advisory only**. **Retain** fresh local enumeration and stability checks, trusted BASE/history, the existing planner, three-way merge/conflict resolution, durable-intent recovery, authoritative executor and full Drive reconciliation. Inventory never authorizes deletion, mutation, BASE, or recovery.

Authority: [Target Specification §7.9](../../../01-target-system/target-system-specification.md), [DEC-339 through DEC-344 plus DEC-362](../../../../authority/decision-register.yaml) and the [reviewed DEC-339 design, especially §§4, 8, 11–12](../../../../research/verified-remote-metadata-inventory/dec339-detailed-architecture-2026-10-10.md). Historical combined DEC-339/DEC-340 review was **REJECT** for DEC-340-01; DEC-344 later made that residual verification-context spoofing risk non-blocking for continued software implementation while preserving genuine PHX-CI/review requirements. Software-only promotion to master did **not** authorize activation or release. **DEC-362 now makes stable-ID existing-file update correction a hard prerequisite to this plan's planner cutover/activation work.**

**Reuse, do not rebuild:**
- **WS-02:** `indexeddb-inventory-storage.ts`, `indexeddb-inventory-authority-storage.ts`, `verified-inventory-integration.ts`, validators and **`verified-local-inventory.ts` (`VerifiedLocalInventoryCoordinator`)**. Local rows must be scoped to the same effective exclusions, refreshed from fresh vault enumeration, and invalidated on local changes/resume. They cannot prove absence or local stability.
- **WS-03:** `inventory-metadata-source.ts`, `verified-metadata-inventory-reader.ts`; use the existing production authenticated `drive.file` boundary, not another OAuth client.
- **WS-05:** `inventory-backed-snapshot-assembler.ts`, `verified-inventory-planning-projection.ts`; retain `snapshot-assembler.ts` for full/recovery fallback.
- **Required prerequisite:** [DEC-362 stable Drive-ID content-update correction](stable-drive-id-content-update-correction-2026-11-18.md) must be implemented, PHX-CI-qualified, independently reviewed and accepted before this plan reaches inventory planner cutover/activation. Ordinary existing-file update and clean-merge propagation must preserve the established `remoteObjectId`; the historical replacement-object candidate/retirement path is not an acceptable baseline.
- **Later, separate gate:** D339-06 `inventory-targeted-convergence-read.ts` remains a **DEC-339 objective**, **deferred but not cancelled**. It may replace an eligible remote verification **read only** after dedicated proof-equivalence, PHX-CI qualification, independent review and Owner authorization. Do not otherwise redesign the authoritative executor lifecycle in this initial integration.

## Owner-directed source integration and final `master` promotion policy (2026-11-21)

**Owner decision — mandatory merge-order rule:** **Do not merge individual DEC-362 tasks, DEC-339 Build Units, or other partially qualified integration work into `master` merely because a unit has passed PHX-CI or an independent review.** Preserve the accepted, frozen source and evidence for each unit on its feature branch. Bring those accepted components together on a separately controlled **DEC-339 integration branch**, qualify the coherent integrated product as a whole, and promote that accepted integrated candidate to `master` **only after all applicable gates below and a distinct Owner merge authorization**. No acceptance, commit, or PR automatically grants that authorization.

The **16 DEC362-01 through DEC362-16 checklist items** describe the stable-ID correction and its prerequisite/integration boundary; they are **not the entire DEC-339 work breakdown**. The accepted DEC-362 software feature and its original source-bound evidence remain valid without piecemeal `master` merges; DEC362-16 requires readiness for downstream integration, **not** an automatic PR merge. Completing those 16 items does not, by itself, satisfy WS-02/WS-03/WS-05/WS-07 integration, operational evidence, or final `master` promotion conditions.

### Required source-assembly order

1. **Finish and independently accept bounded units on their own branches.** For each unit, retain immutable implementation SHA and Git tree, original PHX-CI run/evidence publication, independent blind/final review, accepted minor findings, outstanding dependencies, and current authorized handoff/Owner decision. A merely Builder-qualified or correction-required unit is **not** an integration-ready source.
2. **Keep `master` unchanged by incomplete units.** Do not routinely merge individual unit branches or accepted partial fixes into `master`. Do not use a successful unit test run as justification for a partial `master` merge. Earlier dormant DEC-339 software already on `master` remains historical baseline; this rule governs the remaining integration work rather than retrospectively rewriting history.
3. **Assemble the accepted source on one controlled integration baseline (not `master`).** After the necessary dependent units have been accepted, reconcile/cherry-pick/merge their reviewed source into the designated DEC-339 integration branch in dependency order: accepted DEC-362 same-ID mutation/recovery correction; WS-02 v1/v2 authority, all cursor writers, scope/local-hint safeguards and recovery; WS-03 existing-authenticated-boundary inventory metadata; WS-02/05 isolated shadow publication; WS-05 observation equivalence; and default-off WS-05 runtime composition. The integration Builder must inspect actual current trees rather than copying a historical SHA blindly; verify that accepted semantics survived conflict resolution and that no rejected or unrelated change was carried forward. Preserve source ancestry/accepted references and avoid unnecessary reimplementation.
4. **Qualify the integrated tree, not just its ingredients.** Freeze one combined candidate SHA/tree; run the applicable local pinned PHX-CI build, typecheck, full/focused tests, artifact and repository guards against that **exact assembled source**. Include explicit cross-unit acceptance tests for DEC-362 stable IDs, all canonical cursor and backup/recovery fences, verified Drive metadata continuity, scope/identity invalidation, isolated shadow state, observation/fallback equivalence, and legacy-planner safety. Original per-unit PASS reports remain evidence for those units but **do not substitute** for this combined qualification. Preserve original full output and immutable evidence; **never run GitHub Actions**.
5. **Complete the full DEC-339 gating sequence before requesting `master` promotion.** Finish the remaining steps in the table above, including separate WS-07 direct BVP operational/simulated observations, objective parity/latency/resource evidence, fail-closed negative cases, rollback and migration-readiness proof, independent blind-first integrated source/evidence review, and disposition of blocking findings. Keep inventory activation **default off**; software qualification and merge acceptance do **not** authorize live IndexedDB migration, remote/vault mutations, hardware testing, installation, or release. Any otherwise deferred D339-06 targeted read optimization remains disabled unless its separate gate is later approved.
6. **Request explicit Owner authorization for one controlled final integration merge to `master`.** Present the proposed integration source SHA/tree and target `master` SHA, exact merge direction and full diff, included unit acceptance/evidence register, unresolved findings and risk dispositions, integrated verification results, independent acceptance, rollback strategy, and any outstanding operational/release gates. Obtain the Owner's explicit approval of **that merge** before executing it. If the target branch has advanced or integration conflict resolution changes source, recompute the diff and requalify the actual changed integrated tree as necessary; do not silently promote an unreviewed merge result.
7. **After authorized merge, verify and record the actual resulting `master` tree.** Confirm the correct accepted integrated code, stable-ID ordinary updates, feature-default-off behavior, preserved safety fallbacks, and no accidental inventory activation. Record the merge commit, target/source ancestry, any required post-merge verification evidence and closeout. **Merge, feature activation, live migration, physical testing, deployment and release are separate actions requiring their own applicable Owner authorizations.**

## Non-negotiable stops and release boundary

- **DEC-362 prerequisite:** no inventory planner cutover or activation while ordinary existing-file content update/clean-merge still uses old-ID → replacement-candidate-ID → predecessor-retirement semantics. Same logical file updates preserve its established Google Drive ID.
- The inventory is **device-local**; never sync its rows/cursors as ordinary vault/config files. Same-name siblings and app-scoped `drive.file` visibility never establish global uniqueness or absence.
- Wrong account/root/device/scope, cursor gaps, corruption, ambiguous ancestry, incomplete listings, uncertain effects, pending intents or failed root-wide provenance **block or require proven full fallback**. Do not weaken existing pre-/post-mutation and independent convergence checks.
- Preserve tested **v2-aware legacy-observation rollback** after any approved upgrade. Do not reset unknown state, erase evidence, or assume old binary compatibility.
- **DEC-340-01** remains an uncorrected/deferred verification-integrity finding until independently resolved; PHX-CI development artifact observations are **not** release-approved artifact hashes (DEC-340–343).
- S09/`data.json` integrity and physical acceptance, exact-artifact release approval, live migration/physical testing and installation/release are **independent Owner gates** under DEC-342 and later governing decisions. Recheck their current disposition; this plan does not satisfy or waive them.
- No GitHub Actions, extra planner/executor, second BVP runner, independent verification framework, frozen-core/budget change, or change to historical accepted Phase-2/3/5 software/evidence.

**Ownership:** This is **one WS-05 integration planning task** coordinating WS-02, WS-03, WS-05 and WS-07; not a new workstream or a repeat of D339-01–11 builds. On separately authorized implementation dispatch, divide work into the **fewest execution-sized, dependency-safe Build Units**, bind exact source SHA/writable paths and retain the mandatory Builder→PHX-CI→independent Reviewer sequence. Historical decisions/reviews and unrelated WS-07 state remain intact.
