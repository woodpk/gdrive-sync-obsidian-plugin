### 2.1 Research disposition and approved operating model (2026-11-24)

Owner authorized public research and subsequently agreed with the recommendation to proceed using the Changes API as the normal remote change-detection authority. This resolves the former pre-implementation research hold. The evidence supports reliance on the scoped provider contract, not an assertion of infallible delivery or unrestricted visibility.

| Source | Established finding | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Google [Retrieve changes](https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/manage-changes) and [Track changes](https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/about-changes) | Changes efficiently exposes current changed-item state; Google recommends retaining prior state for comparisons. Entries may be superseded. | Keep per-device ID/parent/revision inventory; replay from durable cursor rather than rediscover unchanged files. No assumption of every intermediate revision. |
| Google [changes.list v3](https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/reference/rest/v3/changes/list) | Supports drive.file, removed entries and opaque non-expiring continuation/terminal tokens. | Complete paginated replay, including empty intermediate pages; atomic terminal cursor publication; handle rejection/error without cursor reset. |
| Google [Identify which change log to track](https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/about-changelogs) | Deletion, loss of user access and corpus moves can look alike; shared-drive changes may require another log. | Never infer intentional deletion from removal; exclude Shared Drives; resolve affected IDs, parent/corpus and exceptional scope changes. |
| Google [OAuth scopes](https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/api-specific-auth) | drive.file limits access to app-authorized files. | Same app/grant binding, invalidate on reauthorization or detected visibility change; no expansion to broad drive scope. |
| [Rclone Drive backend](https://github.com/rclone/rclone/blob/master/backend/drive/drive.go), ChangeNotify/changeNotifyRunner | Independent implementation consumes page tokens, resolves previous paths from cached IDs and new paths from parent IDs, then invalidates affected entries. | Corroborates the efficiency pattern, not proof of BRAIN safety or identical OAuth behavior. |
| [Rclone Drive documentation](https://github.com/rclone/rclone/blob/master/docs/content/drive.md) | Changing OAuth client identity can change which files are visible under drive.file. | Bind app identity and invalidate on relevant grant/app changes. |

Research did not identify substantiated routine omissions of already app-authorized managed-object changes in the supported private personal My Drive case. Public issue tracker #362780334 (https://issuetracker.google.com/issues/362780334), previously identified as concerning deletion identity/path recovery, could not be reopened in the final source check; it is not relied upon as evidence. Search/issue coverage is not exhaustive. Google's explicit inherited-descendant permission event discussion applies to Shared Drives; do not extrapolate it into a universal My Drive/app-visibility guarantee. No live experiment performed. No claim of global absence, linearizable change feed or zero possibility of provider defects.

**Approved normal-path contract P:** for a complete verified app-visible baseline in the supported personal My Drive namespace, stable app/grant and policy binding, and complete scoped Changes replay to H, treat unchanged rows as reusable observation evidence; fresh affected-ID, affected-parent/ancestry and independent final authority/horizon checks establish the planning boundary. This is a reliance on the documented provider contract, not a requirement to mathematically prove every possible provider failure impossible. Cursor equality alone is not proof: consume Changes from the durable cursor on ordinary planning unless an already completed replay in the same fenced attempt supplies that evidence; no double replay after refresh. Both routine root-wide censuses (planning and incremental refresh) MUST be absent on the eligible normal path. Keep whole-root verification at initialization, explicit/scheduled reconciliation, detected or suspected discontinuity, unsupported corpus, uncertain grant/visibility, corrupt/missing state, or when an existing independent execution proof requires it. No broader permissions, no change to executor/convergence/deletion safety, no blanket risk waiver.

Residual risk: a silently omitted event or application-level visibility transition with no observable indication may leave observational metadata stale until a targeted check or periodic reconciliation detects it. Do not claim instantaneous detection of an unobservable condition; full listing also cannot see files outside the grant. Fresh independent effect preconditions and convergence remain unchanged, so cached metadata cannot alone authorize mutation or deletion. When discrepancy is detectable, invalidate/recover/block rather than proceed. If implementation reveals a material additional safety tradeoff, stop that affected optimization and seek Owner decision; no further generic provider-proof research approval gate. Periodic full reconciliation is a required defense, not a claim of immediate equivalence in an out-of-contract provider failure.

## 5. Incremental refresh algorithm

1. Load coherent trusted canonical state/publication and exact lease; validate graph, namespace/coverage and durable authority. Index stable ID, parent→children, normalized occupancy and prior path locally. Never reuse stale lease.
2. Establish fresh pairing and corpus; consume Changes from canonical cursor C (not a new start token), follow complete opaque pagination even empty intermediate pages; unique advancing page tokens, exactly one continuation or terminal, valid IDs/metadata and bounded count/time/retries. Current-state entries may repeat/supersede; conservatively coalesce by ID, preserve old and new affected-scope closure and learned history. Do not assume intermediate events complete. Missing/ambiguous terminal, unknown change type, repeated tokens, failure/cancel/bounds => no publication.
3. Compare changed/new IDs to predecessor. Fresh exact-ID getFile when feed metadata insufficient, unknown provenance/removal/parent; verify changed ancestry to correct domain, cache only same-attempt verified ancestors, require exact marker/root/domain, live kind, one parent, normalized compatible path. Ignore unrelated changes only on affirmative outside-scope evidence. Unknown removed ID, escaped marked object or unclassifiable event => full proof/recovery/block. Do not enumerate foreign/asset trees or fetch their content; if bounded permitted metadata insufficient, block. Never use name to choose identity.
4. Invalidation closure: old/new parent, changed folder/descendants, marker, parent, permission or ownership change, changed root/domain/policy. Ordinary leaf update uses same ID and affected parent occupancy. List affected parent unfiltered, paginated fully, compare actual/candidate ID/name/kind/parent/revision/provenance and duplicate normalized paths. Extra unmarked child, duplicate, missing, inaccessible, revision mismatch, ambiguous subtree or cross-domain/corpus move => no success. F03 may conservatively full rebaseline for folder insertion/move/rename/removal or inherited visibility; never assume unchanged descendants. Avoid querying unaffected remote directories in eligible ordinary path. Deduplicate affected parents/ancestor reads.
5. Removal event, 404/403 or missing metadata is unknown, not deletion. Existing affirmative exact-ID trash proof with known parent/provenance plus trusted history permits existing leaf mask/learned removal semantics; uncertain loss/folder changes rebaseline/block. Preserve ambiguity rather than choose a duplicate name winner.
6. Derive coverage at H from validated complete baseline + complete replay + proven affected scopes; no pretending inherited rows were freshly listed. Explicit proof basis distinguishes inherited continuity from fresh targeted evidence; if current contract cannot distinguish, extend compatibly or retain census. Validate entire local effective graph/ambiguity/digests/bounds. Independently verify final fence and horizon AFTER last provider read; if H drifted discard staging and boundedly retry/replan. Do not rewrite BASE from inventory.
7. Existing VerifiedInventoryBaselineCoordinator.publishIncremental → stage → publishAtomically binds overlays, coverage, receipt, cursor and learned batch via one transaction and compares expected active generation/persistence revision/semantic generation/canonical cursor. Failure preserves prior coherent state or explicit invalid/recovery state; never mixed pointer and cursor. Restart from durable predecessor, preserve pending intents/legacy cursor-writer invalidations, GC respects leases/history. No new publisher.

## 7. Failure and transition table

| Condition | Required result | Forbidden |
|---|---|---|
| Eligible coherent baseline, no new events | reuse with final proof, no generation churn | unconditional cursor equality reuse |
| Complete leaf delta | targeted proof + atomic publication, replan | routine global query in eligible case |
| Advanced cursor, unrelated only | classified durable empty batch/cursor | skip unknown removals/history |
| Partial/malformed/cyclic feed, exhausted retry/cancel | preserve prior authority; defer/block | advance cursor, infer absence |
| Removed/inaccessible/unknown ancestry | affirmative trash proof or recovery/block | infer deletion from 404 |
| Folder, root, grant, shared-drive transition | supported safe full/recovery or block | reuse stale descendants or new corpus |
| Wrong policy/app/device, stale/corrupt generation | invalidate; controlled rebaseline | cross-policy reuse |
| Crash/CAS loser | coherent old state or explicit recovery | split pointer/cursor |
| Explicit disabled mode | unchanged legacy path | touch inventory |
| Full/recovery requested or due | actual physical enumeration | cached substitute |
| Detected violation of P, unsupported or uncertain boundary | retain required census or block, escalate material new tradeoff | treat known uncertainty as trusted |
